# Phase 2 — Candidate Dissections (explicit coordinates)

Construction triangle: right angle at the origin, legs `a ≤ b`. Concrete instance
`a=3, b=4, c=5`, but every cut is a **general line of the figure** so the dissection
generalizes to arbitrary `a,b`. Standard placement:

- Big leg square `B0 = [0,b]×[0,b]` (area `b²`).
- Small leg square `S0 = [b,b+a]×[0,a]` (area `a²`), sharing the corner `(b,0)` region.
- Hyp square `C` = square with corners `(0,0),(b,a),(b−a,a+b),(−a,b)` (area `c²`); its
  edges run **parallel/perpendicular to the hypotenuse** (direction `p=(b,a)`).

All candidates were generated by overlaying a `c`-grid on the Pythagorean tiling
(`verify/explore.py`) and verified for tiling validity (`verify/inspect_candidate.py`,
`verify/verify_dissection.py`). The offset of the `c`-grid is the only free choice.

---

## Candidate K1 — Corner-anchored, 5 pieces, BOTH squares cut (3+2) — **chosen for Phase 4**
**Offset:** `c`-square anchored at the right-angle vertex `(0,0)`.
**Pieces** (with `x0=(a²−ab+b²)/b`, `x1=(a²+b²)/b=c²/b`; for `a=3,b=4`: `x0=3.25`, `x1=6.25`):

Big square `B0` → 3 pieces:
| piece | vertices (general) | `a=3,b=4` | translation (source→hyp) |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 quad | (0,0),(0,b),(x0,b),(b,a) | (0,0),(0,4),(3.25,4),(4,3) | (0, 0) |
| P1 tri  | (0,0),(b,0),(b,a)        | (0,0),(4,0),(4,3)          | (−a, b) = (−3,4) |
| P2 tri  | (b,a),(x0,b),(b,b)       | (4,3),(3.25,4),(4,4)       | (−b, −a) = (−4,−3) |

Small square `S0` → 2 pieces:
| piece | vertices (general) | `a=3,b=4` | translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| P3 quad | (b,a),(b+a,a),(b+a,0),(x1,0) | (4,3),(7,3),(7,0),(6.25,0) | (−(a+b), b−a) = (−7,1) |
| P4 tri  | (x1,0),(b,0),(b,a)           | (6.25,0),(4,0),(4,3)       | (−a, b) = (−3,4) |

**Piece count:** 5 (degenerates to 4 when `a=b`, since P2 vanishes).
**Motion type:** **translation only** (every isometry has rotation part `= I`).
**Cut lines:** in `B0`, segment `(0,0)→(b,a)` (parallel to hypotenuse) and segment
`(b,a)→(x0,b)` (perpendicular to hypotenuse); in `S0`, segment `(b,a)→(x1,0)`
(perpendicular to hypotenuse). All cuts are translates of the hyp-square edges,
anchored at the right-angle vertex `(0,0)` and the junction `(b,a)`.
**Why considered distinctive:** matches the known minimum (5) like Perigal, but cuts
**both** squares (3+2) rather than Perigal's (4 congruent + whole small square).

## Candidate K2 — Perigal (recovered as a sanity check) — known
**Offset:** `c`-grid centred on the big squares (offset `(1.5, 2.0)` for `a=3,b=4`).
Big square → **4 congruent** quadrilaterals (pinwheel through centre), small square
**whole**. **5 pieces = 4+1, translation only.** This is the classic Perigal; the
generator reproduces it exactly, confirming the generator is correct.

## Candidate K3 — Symmetric, 6 pieces, both squares cut (3+3)
**Offset:** `(1.0, −1.0)` for `a=3,b=4`. Big→3, small→3, **translation only**, 6 pieces.
More symmetric but **non-minimal** (6 > 5). A different point of the same family.

## Candidate K4 — 6 pieces (4+2)
**Offset:** `(−0.5, −1.0)`. Big→4, small→2, translation only, 6 pieces. Another
family member, non-minimal.

## Candidate K5 — Thābit-style (rotation), 3 pieces — known
The two leg squares joined at `(b,0)`/`(b,a)`; two hypotenuse-length cuts detach two
triangles congruent to the original, which **rotate** into the hyp square. **3 pieces,
rotation.** Listed for contrast; this is the catalogued Thābit dissection (B1).

## Self-assessment before the gauntlet
- K1, K2, K3, K4 are all **Pythagorean-tessellation-overlay** dissections — i.e.
  members of the **2-parameter family** flagged in `known_dissections.md` (C3). They
  differ only by the grid offset. K2 is literally Perigal.
- K5 is literally Thābit.
- The only candidate worth taking to a rigorous write-up is **K1** (clean, minimal
  piece count, distinct *from the classic Perigal cut*). But its family membership is a
  red flag the collision gauntlet must confront head-on.

Chosen for Phase 3–4: **K1**, with eyes open about C3.
