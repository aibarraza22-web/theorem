## 2. Skeptical referee — "this is just a Perigal/tessellation relabeling"

**Referee's thesis.** *You overlaid the standard `c`-grid on the standard Pythagorean
tiling and slid it to the corner. cut-the-knot already documents the full 2-parameter
family of these overlays, explicitly noting Perigal is one special case. Your "both
squares cut, 3+2" is just another offset in that family. The cut directions (∥/⊥ to the
hypotenuse), the motion (translation), and the generating method are all identical to
the catalogued family. Nothing here is outside the catalog.*

**My answer — the referee is right.** Applying the stated equivalence test:
- **Same generating method:** K1 *is* a `c`-grid overlay on the Pythagorean tiling
  (`verify/explore.py` literally constructs it that way). The family C3 is, by its own
  description, *all* such overlays. Membership in a catalogued parametric family is the
  catalog entry. → K1 is catalogued.
- **The one honest distinction** — K1 ≠ the *classic Perigal picture* (different piece
  shapes: 3+2, not 4+1) — only demotes K1 from "identical to Perigal" to "VARIANT of
  Perigal." It does **not** lift K1 out of family C3.
- I **cannot** answer the referee's core point, so per the mission I **downgrade**:
  from any "addable" hope to **DUPLICATE (family-level)**.

**Residual honesty.** Two facts keep this from being an airtight DUPLICATE proof and
keep a sliver of doubt, both recorded:
1. cut-the-knot pages were **unreachable (403)**; the 2-parameter-family claim rests on
   a search snippet, not the page itself.
2. The full print catalog (Loomis's 367 in full text, Frederickson's book) was not
   searchable.
Neither doubt points *toward* novelty — both are about whether the duplicate is
"Perigal-family on cut-the-knot" vs. "also elsewhere." The honest call remains
**DUPLICATE / not addable**.

## 3. Why this is still a useful (negative) deliverable

The collision gauntlet worked exactly as intended: a candidate that *looked*
distinctive (matches the minimum count 5, yet cuts both squares unlike Perigal) was
shown, by an actual search, to be a member of an **explicitly catalogued 2-parameter
family**. The lesson, recorded in `LOG.md`: **clean translation dissections of the
Pythagorean configuration collapse into the tessellation-superposition family**, and
low-piece rotation dissections collapse into Zhao/Bhaskara/Thābit — so the room for a
genuinely uncatalogued *direct* dissection is very small, and this particular attempt
does not occupy it. The rigorous, machine-checked construction (general `a,b`) is kept
as a correct—if not new—worked dissection.
